Why people switch to Karvaan

Honestly, most of it is just doing the basics properly and not cutting corners on freshness.

Roasted to order, in small batches

We don't roast a month's stock in one go. Small batches mean we can actually taste every roast before it goes out — and reject the ones that aren't right.

The roast date is printed, not hidden

Every bag has the roast date on the front. If your coffee was roasted more than 10 days before dispatch, it doesn't ship. Simple rule, no exceptions.

Sourced with a paper trail

Our lots come through two importers we've worked with since 2021. Farm, altitude and harvest year are listed on each product — no vague "premium blend" language.

Ground how you actually brew

Moka pot, French press, V60, espresso — pick your method at checkout and we grind for that. Or take it whole bean, which we'd recommend if you have a grinder.

How a subscription works

Three steps, and you can change almost everything later from your account page.

  1. 1

    Pick your coffee

    Choose a bestseller, or pick "Roaster's choice" if you'd rather be surprised — we rotate a different single origin each delivery.

  2. 2

    Tell us how often

    Every week, every two weeks, or monthly. Most households of two go through a 250g bag in about 10 days, for reference.

  3. 3

    We roast and post it

    Your coffee is roasted on the next roast day, rested for a day, then dispatched. Pause, skip or cancel anytime — no phone call needed.
